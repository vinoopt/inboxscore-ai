My recommendation
Concept 3 · Signal — strongest brand story
Concept 1 (Slab) is safe and modernist but reads as "letters in a box" — same trap as the current "IS". It's an upgrade in execution but not in identity.

Concept 2 (Mono) is clean and dev-tool-y but the "I" is so thin it disappears at 16×16. It also leans technical when our buyer is more often a marketer or founder.

Concept 3 (Signal) is the only one where the mark says something: I + ascending bars = "Inbox Score." The green dot on the tallest bar carries the dashboard's brand colour. Reads at favicon size, scales beautifully, and tells anyone who glances at the tab what we do.
If you go with Concept 3, sweep covers: favicon (replacing 📨 emoji), all 5 marketing/auth topnavs, all 8 app page topnavs, plus the og-image.png reference. Single SVG used everywhere via data:image/svg+xml,<svg…> — no extra HTTP request.
